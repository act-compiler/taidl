# Operations

An operation word names an XLA-HLO operation, with the words of its name joined by underscores, and StableHLO has an operation of the same name unless the table says otherwise.
`dot` and `broadcast` follow XLA-HLO's names, where StableHLO has `dot_general` and `broadcast_in_dim`.
`reduce_<reducer>` is TAIDL's own form: a `reduce` with its reducer fixed.

## What an operation takes

Each operation word takes the operands and the attributes its row gives: how many values, or the one operand of `parameter` and `constant`, and each attribute's name, its form, and whether a line must give it.
A row holds what XLA-HLO's text parser checks without a shape, and StableHLO's rule or this language's own where XLA-HLO's text leaves one open: `dot`'s 2 operands, `pad`'s three attributes and a required `transpose_a` on `triangular_solve` are StableHLO's, and `dot`'s required contracting lists are this language's own.
An integer is a literal of either sign or an expression in backticks, a list is a brace list and may be empty, and a number is any number literal.
An attribute in no row is refused: one whose value no form writes, such as `result_accuracy`, or one that says how an operation runs rather than what it computes, such as `operand_precision` or `indices_are_sorted`.
A `dot` gives both its contracting lists, either of which may be empty, and a batch list it leaves out is empty.

## Operations that stay out

Of the operations of XLA-HLO and StableHLO that one line can express, one result of a type and a shape the text gives computed from operands and attributes of the forms `semantics.md` gives, the grammar leaves out `ragged_dot` and `scaled_dot`, which XLA-HLO alone has and which, unlike the elementwise operations only XLA-HLO has, each take operands of their own, group sizes or scales, that no StableHLO operation takes; an operation that says how a program runs rather than what it computes; and an operation StableHLO's specification leaves out.
The others stay out because a line cannot carry them.
By kind:

- An operation that takes a body, a comparator or a callee, which a line cannot carry: `reduce` with a reducer of its own, `reduce_window`, `scatter`, `sort`, `select_and_scatter`, `map`, `while`, `conditional` (StableHLO's `if` and `case`), `call`, `fusion`, `scan`, `composite` and `custom_call`.
- `convolution`: its window and its dimension labels need syntax the grammar lacks.
- Complex numbers, random numbers and quantized values, as in `complex`, `fft`, `rng`, `stochastic_convert` and `uniform_quantize`: a semantics has no such type and no random state.
- An operation with several results, such as `batch_norm_training`, `topk` or `tuple`: a line defines one value.
- An operation on a shape known only at run time, such as `dynamic_reshape` or `get_dimension_size`: the text gives every shape.
- An operation that orders effects, reaches another device or serves the compiler alone, such as `after_all`, `all_reduce`, `send`, `optimization_barrier` or XLA-HLO's `bitcast`: it says how a program runs, not what an instruction computes.
- An operation StableHLO alone has and its specification leaves out, such as `einsum` or `torch_index_select`: it is on its way out of the IR.
